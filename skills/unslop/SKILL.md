# Make existing prose concrete

Preserve the author's meaning, language, technical claims, and uncertainty. Change phrasing, not the conclusion or authorization.

- Lead with the outcome or action the reader needs.
- Delete preambles, self-praise, generic conclusions, and sentences that add no information.
- Replace abstract words with the mechanism, actor, measured value, or concrete action.
- Prefer familiar words, active voice, and sentences that do not require rereading.
- Remove rhetorical question-and-answer pairs, unrequested contrasts, repeated summary headings, and forced metaphors.
- Keep qualifications that limit a claim. Do not turn an inference into a fact or an unrun check into a successful result.
- Keep lists and headings when they help the reader navigate real structure. Avoid formatting that gives a short answer the appearance of a report.

Use natural Chinese for Chinese material and preserve code, URLs, flags, and identifiers exactly unless an independently verified correction is part of the task. Return the edited text directly unless the user asks for an explanation of edits. Do not write it to a file or external app unless saving is authorized.
